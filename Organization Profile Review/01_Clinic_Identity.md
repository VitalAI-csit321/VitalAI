# Clinic Identity

## Clinic Overview

**Clinic Name:** GreenCare Family Medical Clinic
**ABN:** 83 456 729 114

GreenCare Family Medical Clinic is a community general practice in central Sydney. It provides primary healthcare and the administrative services that surround it: booking, registration, referrals, prescription request coordination, and record handling. The clinic runs an in-person practice supported by a secure digital system with role-based access control, so patient requests move to the right staff member without exposing data to the wrong role.

The clinic serves families, working adults, pensioners, and concession holders. Extended Thursday hours exist to support patients who cannot attend during standard business hours.

## Services Offered

All care is delivered in person. The clinic provides general practice care and its administrative support:

- General consultations, standard and long
- Appointment scheduling and management
- Patient onboarding and registration
- Referral processing and document handling
- Prescription request coordination (administrative intake only, doctor approves)
- Follow-up appointment reminders
- Medical record organisation and administrative support
- Childhood and adult immunisations
- Health assessments (65 and over, and new patient health checks)
- GP Management Plans and Team Care Arrangements for chronic conditions
- Mental Health Treatment Plans
- Skin checks
- Antenatal shared care
- Home Medicine Reviews for eligible aged care patients
- On-site pathology collection (Tuesday and Thursday mornings, via visiting collector)

VitalAI handles the administrative side of these services: booking the right appointment type, routing the request, and keeping records organised. It does not provide clinical advice, interpret results, or approve prescriptions. Those actions belong to the treating doctor.

## Billing Information

GreenCare operates a mixed billing model.

**Medicare (bulk billing):** Available for eligible patients, including children under 16, pensioners, concession card holders, and Health Care Card holders. No out-of-pocket cost for these patients on standard consultations.

**Private billing:** Standard consultation fees apply to other patients, payable at the time of service.

## Referral Process

Patients may request referrals through the front desk in person, by phone, or through the online request system. Requests are reviewed by administrative staff and forwarded to the appropriate doctor. Once a doctor approves, the referral is prepared and recorded, and the patient is notified by email or phone. Standard referrals are processed within 3 business days. Urgent referrals are prioritised and escalated on the same day.
