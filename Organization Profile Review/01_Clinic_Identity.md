# Clinic Identity

## Clinic Overview

**Clinic Name:** VitalAI Clinic
**ABN:** 83 456 729 114

VitalAI Clinic is a community general practice on the University of Wollongong's Wollongong campus. It provides primary healthcare and the administrative services that surround it: booking, registration, referrals, prescription request coordination, and record handling. The clinic runs an in-person practice supported by a secure digital system with role-based access control, so patient requests move to the right staff member without exposing data to the wrong role.

The clinic is open to everyone. A patient does not need to be a student or a staff member of the university. It serves students, university staff, families, working adults, pensioners, and concession holders.

## Doctor Availability

All doctors consult Monday to Friday within the clinic's opening hours. The days and times a particular doctor is free change from week to week, so the clinic does not publish a fixed timetable for each doctor.

A patient who asks for a particular doctor is offered that doctor's next available times when they ask to book. A patient with no preference is offered their own regular doctor first, and then any doctor with a free time.

Each doctor manages their own calendar. No doctor can view another doctor's calendar in the clinic's system. Clinical work happens outside the system, which holds the administrative layer only.

## Services Offered

All care is delivered in person. The clinic provides general practice care and its administrative support:

- General consultations, standard and long
- Appointment scheduling and management
- Patient onboarding and registration
- Referral processing and document handling
- Prescription request coordination (administrative intake only, doctor approves)
- Follow-up appointment reminders
- Medical record organisation and administrative support
- Childhood and adult immunisations, including the seasonal influenza vaccine
- Travel health consultations and travel vaccinations
- Health assessments (65 and over, and new patient health checks)
- GP Management Plans and Team Care Arrangements for chronic conditions
- Mental Health Treatment Plans
- Sexual and reproductive health, including contraception advice and cervical screening
- Skin checks and minor skin procedures
- Antenatal shared care
- Home Medicine Reviews for eligible aged care patients
- Medical certificates, issued by a doctor during a consultation
- On-site pathology collection (Tuesday and Thursday mornings, via visiting collector)

The clinic's system handles the administrative side of these services: booking the right appointment type, routing the request, and keeping records organised. It does not provide clinical advice, interpret results, or approve prescriptions. Those actions belong to the treating doctor.

## Billing Information

VitalAI Clinic operates a mixed billing model.

**Medicare (bulk billing):** Available for eligible patients only: children under 16, pensioners, concession card holders, and Health Care Card holders. No out-of-pocket cost for these patients on standard consultations. Other adults with a Medicare card are not bulk billed. Being a university student does not by itself make a patient eligible for bulk billing; a Health Care Card or concession card does.

**Private billing:** Standard consultation fees apply to all other patients, payable at the time of service. A patient with a Medicare card receives the Medicare rebate back and pays the gap.

**International students and overseas visitors:** Seen as private patients and pay the full fee at the time of service. A student with Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) can claim part of the fee back from their OSHC insurer using the clinic's receipt. The clinic does not bill OSHC or travel insurers directly.

## Referral Process

Patients may request referrals through the front desk in person, by phone, or by email. Requests are reviewed by administrative staff and forwarded to the appropriate doctor. Once a doctor approves, the referral is prepared and recorded, and the patient is notified by email or phone. Standard referrals are processed within 3 business days. Urgent referrals are prioritised and escalated on the same day.
