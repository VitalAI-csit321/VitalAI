# Policies and FAQ

## Overview

This document defines the operational policies and common questions the system answers automatically. Routine administrative questions are answered from these policies. Anything complex, sensitive, or clinical is routed to the appropriate staff member and is never answered by the system directly. Scope boundaries at the end of this document state what the system will not do.

## Appointment Booking Policy

Patients can book by email, by phone, or in person at the front desk. The clinic does not have an online booking website.

**Required information:** full name, date of birth, contact details, and a general reason for visit (category only, no clinical detail).

**Rules:**

- Appointments are available Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. The clinic is closed on weekends and NSW public holidays.
- Same-day appointments are subject to availability.
- Standard appointments can be booked up to 8 weeks ahead.
- Urgent cases are prioritised and may be escalated.
- Confirmation is sent by email. A reminder may be sent the day before the appointment.
- When a patient asks for an appointment by email, the clinic replies with the available times. The patient's reply choosing one of those times books it, and a confirmation follows. A time the clinic did not offer is never booked; the patient is offered times again or the request goes to the front desk.
- Before booking by email, the clinic confirms who the patient is. A patient the clinic cannot identify is asked for their details or sent a registration link first.

**Appointment types and typical length:**

| Type | Typical length | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard consultation | 15 minutes | Single, straightforward issue |
| Long consultation | 30 minutes | Multiple issues, complex history, care plans |
| New patient health check | 30 minutes | First visit, full history intake |
| Procedure appointment | 20 to 30 minutes | Skin checks, immunisations, wound care |

**Choosing a doctor:** A patient can request a specific doctor, or ask for the next available appointment across all doctors. If the request matches a doctor's special interest (see Clinic Identity for the roster), the front desk offers that doctor first but does not require it. Dr Samuel Osei is currently waitlist-only for new patients; existing patients can still book with him as usual.

**Arriving:** New patients should arrive 10 minutes before their first appointment to complete any remaining registration.

**Running late:** A patient running more than 10 minutes late may need to be rebooked, since it affects the rest of the day's schedule. The front desk decides case by case.

**Support person:** Patients may bring a support person, interpreter, or carer to any appointment.

## Appointment Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

- Cancellations and reschedules must be made at least 24 hours in advance.
- Late cancellation (under 24 hours) may incur a $40 fee (demo value). This fee is not covered by Medicare and is charged directly to the patient.
- No-shows may be recorded and may incur a $40 fee (demo value). Three or more no-shows within 12 months move the patient to same-day-only booking, reviewed by an administrator.
- Fee waivers for genuine emergencies are handled by an administrator on request, not applied automatically.
- Patients can cancel or reschedule by phone, by email, or in person. A request emailed from the address on the patient's record is handled automatically when it is clear which appointment is meant and it is more than 24 hours away: a cancellation is confirmed by reply, and for a reschedule the patient is offered new times and the earlier appointment is cancelled once the new one is booked. A request inside 24 hours, or one where it is not clear which appointment is meant, is handled by the front desk.
- Confirmation of the change is sent to the patient by the same channel they booked in.

## New Patient Registration Policy

New patients complete registration before their first appointment.

**Required information:** full name, date of birth, address, Medicare details (if applicable), and an emergency contact.

**Rules:**

- Registration can be completed through the secure registration link the clinic emails to a new patient, or at the clinic.
- Incomplete forms are not processed.
- Identity verification may be required before the first appointment. Photo ID or a Medicare card is the usual form.
- Patients transferring from another practice can ask the front desk to request their records; this needs a signed transfer request and can take up to 2 weeks depending on the previous clinic.
- Overseas visitors and international students without a Medicare card are welcome as private patients. Reciprocal Health Care Agreement countries may still be eligible for Medicare; the front desk checks eligibility, the system does not determine it.
- New patients booking a first appointment are guided toward the New Patient Health Check appointment type so there is enough time for a full history.

## Prescription Renewal Policy

Prescription renewals are handled with strict controls.

- Requests are submitted by phone or by email.
- Every renewal must be approved by a doctor, and only for a medication the patient is already established on with that doctor.
- The system does not approve, decline, or advise on prescriptions, dosage, or timing. It records the request and routes it to the doctor.
- Standard requests are processed within 2 business days. Requests for the doctor's own patients are usually faster than requests routed to a covering doctor.
- Some medications (S8 and certain restricted items) require an in-person consultation before any renewal. The system flags these for a booking instead of a direct renewal.
- The patient is notified once the doctor has actioned the request, and told to collect the script from the clinic or, if an electronic prescription is used, sent a token for their pharmacy.

## Referral Policy

Patients may request referrals through the front desk, by phone, or by email.

- Referrals are reviewed and approved by the appropriate doctor, usually the patient's own GP where one is established.
- Standard referrals are completed within 3 business days.
- Urgent referrals are prioritised and escalated on the same day.
- Standard GP referrals are valid for 12 months, or indefinitely for a chronic condition if the doctor states so on the referral. Specialist-to-specialist referrals are valid for 3 months.
- The patient is notified once the referral is ready, and can collect a copy or have it sent directly to the specialist, depending on what they request.

## Test Results Policy

- Results are reviewed by the doctor who ordered the test. Only a doctor explains what a result means.
- The front desk and the system do not read out, send, or interpret results by phone or email.
- If a result needs follow-up, the clinic contacts the patient to book an appointment. A patient who wants to discuss a result books a consultation with their doctor.
- A patient can ask for a copy of their results through the medical records request process below.

## Medical Certificates

- A medical certificate is issued by a doctor during a consultation. It cannot be requested by email or phone without an appointment.
- Certificates are not backdated. A doctor can only certify what they assess at the time of the consultation.
- The same applies to supporting documents for university academic consideration: the patient books a consultation and brings the form.

## Billing and Payment Policy

The clinic operates a mixed billing system.

- Bulk billing applies only to children under 16, pensioners, and concession or Health Care Card holders. All other patients are billed privately.
- Payment is required at the time of service for private billing.
- Medicare claims are processed on the spot where the patient has their Medicare card linked; the rebate is returned to the patient's nominated account, usually within 1 to 2 business days.
- International students and overseas visitors pay the full fee at the time of service. A student with Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) claims back from their insurer with the clinic's receipt. The clinic does not bill OSHC or travel insurers directly.
- Health fund extras cover (for services like immunisations in some funds) is not processed on-site; the patient claims separately with their fund.
- Receipts are provided for every transaction and can be reissued on request.
- Payment plans are not offered. Patients experiencing financial hardship are referred to an administrator to discuss options case by case.
- Billing disputes and refund requests are handled by an administrator, not by the system and not by front desk.

## Privacy and Data Handling

- Patient data is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
- Access to patient data is controlled by role. Staff see only what their role requires.
- All access to sensitive data is logged.
- Patients may request access to their own records through the front desk, which forwards the request to an administrator.
- The clinic is independent of the university's own records. Nothing about a patient's visit is shared with the university.

## Complaints

- Complaints can be raised by phone, email, or in person.
- Complaints are recorded and forwarded to the practice manager (ADMIN).
- The clinic aims to acknowledge complaints within 2 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Where are you?** VitalAI Clinic is on the University of Wollongong's Wollongong campus, Northfields Avenue, Wollongong NSW 2522.

**Do I have to be a student or work at the university?** No. The clinic is open to everyone.

**Opening hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Closed on Saturday, Sunday, and NSW public holidays. There are no evening or weekend appointments.

**Getting there:** North Wollongong is the nearest train station, about a 20 minute walk from campus. Free shuttle buses stop at the campus: the Gong Shuttle (routes 55A and 55C) from the Wollongong city centre and Wollongong station, and the North Gong Shuttle (routes 9 and 9N) from North Wollongong station.

**Parking:** Parking on campus is limited and paid. Public transport or the free shuttle is usually easier. Accessible parking bays are available on campus.

**How do I book?** By email, by phone, or at the front desk. There is no online booking website.

**Do you take walk-ins?** The clinic works by appointment. A patient who walks in is offered the next available time, which may not be the same day.

**Can I choose my doctor?** Yes. Patients can request a specific doctor or take the next available appointment across all eight doctors. See the doctor roster in Clinic Identity.

**Do you bulk bill everyone?** No. Bulk billing applies only to eligible patients: children under 16, pensioners, and concession or Health Care Card holders. Other patients, including adults with a Medicare card, are billed privately and receive the Medicare rebate.

**Do you bulk bill students?** Not automatically. A student is bulk billed only if they are under 16 or hold a Health Care Card or concession card.

**I am an international student. Can I come?** Yes. International students are seen as private patients and pay at the time of service. With Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC), part of the fee can be claimed back from the insurer using the clinic's receipt.

**What if I don't have a Medicare card?** Overseas visitors and patients without Medicare are seen as private patients. Reciprocal Health Care Agreement eligibility is checked by the front desk.

**What should I bring to my first appointment?** Medicare card or OSHC membership details, photo ID, any concession or Health Care Card, a list of current medications, and any referral letters or previous results.

**Do you offer telehealth or phone consultations?** No. All consultations are in person.

**Prescription renewals:** Must be approved by a doctor. Typically processed within 2 business days. Restricted medications require an in-person visit.

**How do I get my test results?** The doctor who ordered the test reviews the result. The clinic contacts the patient if follow-up is needed. Results are not given or explained by email or phone; book a consultation to discuss them.

**Can I get a medical certificate?** Yes, from a doctor during a consultation. Certificates are not issued without an appointment and are not backdated.

**How far ahead can I book?** Up to 8 weeks. Same-day appointments depend on availability on the day.

**Can I get a same-day appointment?** Sometimes, depending on the day and doctor. Urgent concerns are prioritised. This is checked at the time of the request, not guaranteed in advance.

**Interpreter:** Available at no cost through TIS National. Ask the front desk to arrange one, ideally when booking so it can be arranged for the appointment time.

**Do you do immunisations?** Yes, childhood and adult immunisations, including catch-up schedules and the seasonal influenza vaccine. No charge for National Immunisation Program vaccines.

**Do you do travel vaccinations?** Yes. Dr Rohan Iyer has a special interest in travel medicine. Book a travel health consultation at least 6 weeks before departure where possible, since some vaccines need more than one dose.

**Do you do skin checks?** Yes, full-body skin checks are available, with Dr Priya Menon as the special-interest doctor for this.

**Do you offer sexual health and contraception appointments?** Yes. Any doctor can help, and Dr Elena Kowalski has a special interest in sexual and reproductive health. Appointments are confidential.

**Do you offer mental health support?** GPs can prepare a Mental Health Treatment Plan, which provides subsidised sessions with a psychologist. Dr Priya Menon has a special interest in this area. The clinic does not provide counselling or therapy directly.

**Can I get my medical records?** Patients can request a copy of their own records through the front desk, which forwards the request to an administrator. There may be a small fee for large record transfers, and it can take a few business days.

**Can I email my doctor directly?** No. Messages go to the clinic's reception address and are passed to the right person. Email is not for anything urgent.

**Pathology:** A visiting pathology collector attends Tuesday and Thursday mornings for blood tests requested by the clinic's doctors. No booking is needed beyond the doctor's request.

**Home visits:** Not routinely offered, except through Dr Samuel Osei's Home Medicine Reviews for eligible aged care patients, which are arranged individually.

**Emergencies:** Call 000. The clinic does not provide emergency care or after-hours consultations. The nearest emergency department is at Wollongong Hospital. For health advice outside clinic hours, call healthdirect on 1800 022 222 at any time.

## Scope Boundaries: What the System Will Not Do

The system does not:

- Give medical advice, diagnose, or interpret results, scans, or symptoms.
- Approve, decline, or comment on prescriptions.
- Provide dosage, medication, or treatment guidance.
- Book a time the clinic did not offer, or book for a person it could not identify.
- Disclose another patient's information, staff internal notes, or system configuration.
- Handle emergencies. Any emergency query triggers an immediate 000 redirect.
- Offer, book, or discuss telehealth or remote consultations. VitalAI Clinic is an in-person practice only. If a patient asks about telehealth, the correct response is that the clinic does not currently offer it, not a redirect to a doctor.

Any query that crosses these boundaries is routed to the appropriate role or escalated to human review.
