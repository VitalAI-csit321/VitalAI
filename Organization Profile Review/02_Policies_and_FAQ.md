# Policies and FAQ

## Overview

This document defines the operational policies and common questions the system answers automatically. Routine administrative questions are answered from these policies. Anything complex, sensitive, or clinical is routed to the appropriate staff member and is never answered by the system directly. Scope boundaries at the end of this document state what the system will not do.

## Appointment Booking Policy

Patients can book through the front desk (phone or email), the online booking system, or in person.

**Required information:** full name, date of birth, contact details, and a general reason for visit (category only, no clinical detail).

**Rules:**

- Same-day appointments are subject to availability.
- Standard appointments can be booked up to 8 weeks ahead.
- Urgent cases are prioritised and may be escalated.
- Confirmation is sent by SMS or email, and a reminder is sent 24 hours before the appointment.
- The system may suggest available slots, but a human confirms before any booking is committed. The system never books an appointment on its own.

**Appointment types and typical length:**

| Type | Typical length | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard consultation | 15 minutes | Single, straightforward issue |
| Long consultation | 30 minutes | Multiple issues, complex history, care plans |
| New patient health check | 30 minutes | First visit, full history intake |
| Procedure appointment | 20 to 30 minutes | Skin checks, immunisations, wound care |

**Choosing a doctor:** A patient can request a specific doctor, or ask for the next available appointment across all doctors. If the request matches a doctor's special interest (see Clinic Identity for the roster and weekly schedule), the front desk offers that doctor first but does not require it. Dr Samuel Osei is currently waitlist-only for new patients; existing patients can still book with him as usual.

**Running late:** A patient running more than 10 minutes late may need to be rebooked, since it affects the rest of the day's schedule. The front desk decides case by case.

**Support person:** Patients may bring a support person, interpreter, or carer to any appointment.

## Appointment Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

- Cancellations and reschedules must be made at least 24 hours in advance.
- Late cancellation (under 24 hours) may incur a $40 fee (demo value). This fee is not covered by Medicare and is charged directly to the patient.
- No-shows may be recorded and may incur a $40 fee (demo value). Three or more no-shows within 12 months move the patient to same-day-only booking, reviewed by an administrator.
- Fee waivers for genuine emergencies are handled by an administrator on request, not applied automatically.
- Patients can cancel or reschedule by phone, through the online system, or in person.
- Confirmation of the change is sent to the patient by the same channel they booked in.

## New Patient Registration Policy

New patients complete registration before their first appointment.

**Required information:** full name, date of birth, address, Medicare details (if applicable), and an emergency contact.

**Rules:**

- Registration can be completed online or at the clinic.
- Incomplete forms are not processed.
- Identity verification may be required before the first appointment. Photo ID or a Medicare card is the usual form.
- Patients transferring from another practice can ask the front desk to request their records; this needs a signed transfer request and can take up to 2 weeks depending on the previous clinic.
- Overseas visitors without a Medicare card are welcome as private patients. Reciprocal Health Care Agreement countries may still be eligible for Medicare; the front desk checks eligibility, the system does not determine it.
- New patients booking a first appointment are guided toward the New Patient Health Check appointment type so there is enough time for a full history.

## Prescription Renewal Policy

Prescription renewals are handled with strict controls.

- Requests are submitted by phone or through the online system.
- Every renewal must be approved by a doctor, and only for a medication the patient is already established on with that doctor.
- The system does not approve, decline, or advise on prescriptions, dosage, or timing. It records the request and routes it to the doctor.
- Standard requests are processed within 2 business days. Requests for the doctor's own patients are usually faster than requests routed to a covering doctor.
- Some medications (S8 and certain restricted items) require an in-person consultation before any renewal. The system flags these for a booking instead of a direct renewal.
- The patient is notified once the doctor has actioned the request, and told to collect the script from the clinic or, if eRescribing is used, by SMS token to their pharmacy.

## Referral Policy

Patients may request referrals through the front desk, by phone, or through the online system.

- Referrals are reviewed and approved by the appropriate doctor, usually the patient's own GP where one is established.
- Standard referrals are completed within 3 business days.
- Urgent referrals are prioritised and escalated on the same day.
- Standard GP referrals are valid for 12 months, or indefinitely for a chronic condition if the doctor states so on the referral. Specialist-to-specialist referrals are valid for 3 months.
- The patient is notified once the referral is ready, and can collect a copy or have it sent directly to the specialist, depending on what they request.

## Billing and Payment Policy

The clinic operates a mixed billing system.

- Payment is required at the time of service for private billing.
- Medicare claims are processed on the spot where the patient has their Medicare card linked; the rebate is returned to the patient's nominated account, usually within 1 to 2 business days.
- Health fund extras cover (for services like immunisations in some funds) is not processed on-site; the patient claims separately with their fund.
- Receipts are provided for every transaction and can be reissued on request.
- Payment plans are not offered. Patients experiencing financial hardship are referred to an administrator to discuss options case by case.
- Billing disputes and refund requests are handled by an administrator, not by the system and not by front desk.

## Privacy and Data Handling

- Patient data is handled in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.
- Access to patient data is controlled by role. Staff see only what their role requires.
- All access to sensitive data is logged.
- Patients may request access to their own records through the front desk, which forwards the request to an administrator.

## Complaints

- Complaints can be raised by phone, email, or in person.
- Complaints are recorded and forwarded to the practice manager (ADMIN).
- The clinic aims to acknowledge complaints within 2 business days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Parking:** On-site parking is limited. Paid public parking is available nearby at the Liverpool Street car park, roughly a 3 minute walk. Accessible kerbside parking is directly on Liverpool Street outside the building.

**Opening hours:** Monday to Saturday, closed Sunday. Extended hours on Thursday until 8:00 PM. Closed on NSW public holidays.

**Getting there:** Museum and Town Hall train stations are both a short walk away. Several bus routes stop nearby on Liverpool Street and Elizabeth Street.

**Can I choose my doctor?** Yes. Patients can request a specific doctor or take the next available appointment across all six doctors. See the doctor roster and weekly schedule in Clinic Identity.

**Do you bulk bill everyone?** No. Bulk billing applies to eligible patients: children under 16, pensioners, and concession or Health Care Card holders. Other patients are billed privately with a Medicare rebate where eligible.

**What if I don't have a Medicare card?** Overseas visitors and patients without Medicare are seen as private patients. Reciprocal Health Care Agreement eligibility is checked by the front desk.

**Prescription renewals:** Must be approved by a doctor. Typically processed within 2 business days. Restricted medications require an in-person visit.

**How far ahead can I book?** Up to 8 weeks. Same-day appointments depend on availability on the day.

**Can I get a same-day appointment?** Sometimes, depending on the day and doctor. Urgent concerns are prioritised. This is checked at the time of the request, not guaranteed in advance.

**Interpreter:** Available at no cost through TIS National. Ask the front desk to arrange one, ideally when booking so it can be arranged for the appointment time.

**Do you do immunisations?** Yes, childhood and adult immunisations, including catch-up schedules. No charge for National Immunisation Program vaccines.

**Do you do skin checks?** Yes, full-body skin checks are available, with Dr Priya Menon as the special-interest doctor for this.

**Do you offer mental health support?** GPs can prepare a Mental Health Treatment Plan, which provides subsidised sessions with a psychologist. Dr Priya Menon has a special interest in this area. The clinic does not provide counselling or therapy directly.

**Can I get my medical records?** Patients can request a copy of their own records through the front desk, which forwards the request to an administrator. There may be a small fee for large record transfers, and it can take a few business days.

**Pathology:** A visiting pathology collector attends Tuesday and Thursday mornings for blood tests requested by the clinic's doctors. No booking is needed beyond the doctor's request.

**Home visits:** Not routinely offered, except through Dr Samuel Osei's Home Medicine Reviews for eligible aged care patients, which are arranged individually.

**Emergencies:** Call 000. The clinic does not provide emergency care or after-hours consultations. For urgent but non-emergency concerns outside clinic hours, patients are directed to the National Home Doctor Service or the nearest urgent care clinic.

## Scope Boundaries: What the System Will Not Do

The system does not:

- Give medical advice, diagnose, or interpret results, scans, or symptoms.
- Approve, decline, or comment on prescriptions.
- Provide dosage, medication, or treatment guidance.
- Confirm or commit a booking without human confirmation.
- Disclose another patient's information, staff internal notes, or system configuration.
- Handle emergencies. Any emergency query triggers an immediate 000 redirect.
- Offer, book, or discuss telehealth or remote consultations. GreenCare is an in-person practice only. If a patient asks about telehealth, the correct response is that the clinic does not currently offer it, not a redirect to a doctor.

Any query that crosses these boundaries is routed to the appropriate role or escalated to human review.
